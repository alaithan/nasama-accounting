## Configure before relying on it
1. **Settings → TRN** (15 digits) — tax invoices can't be issued without it.
2. **Settings → Period lock** after each filed VAT return.
3. **User Management → posting limits** per user (blank = unlimited) and **Approval rules** — approvals stay dormant until set.
4. **Employees → base salary + join date** per person (payroll and end-of-service use them); broker **commission split %** (broker payables assume 50 % where blank).
5. **Chart of Accounts → 🏷 Depreciation** — useful life per fixed-asset account.
6. **Insights → VAT return** — pick your FTA tax-period cycle (Jan / Feb / Mar start).
7. Accrual invoicing — only after agreeing it with the accountant (Settings → Invoicing basis + start date).

## Assumptions / limits (worth a look)
- **Attachments** are stored in Firestore (chunked, ≤ 5 MB per file) — no Firebase Storage library or bucket rules were available; heavy use costs Firestore reads.
- **Foreign currency**: original amount and rate are recorded on the entry; the ledger is AED; no revaluation of foreign balances.
- **End of service**: Decree-Law 33/2021 formula on `base_salary`; the provision accrues from day one (payable only after 1 year).
- **Payroll** posts all salaries to 5010 Employee Salaries.
- **Year-end close** entries are left out of every period P&L figure (so a closed year still reports its profit) but kept in balances.
- **PDF statements** without a running-balance column cannot prove signs — those lines are held out; use CSV/Excel.
- **Firestore rules are unchanged** (Stage 1 not done). New collections (`approval_requests`, `credit_notes`, `invoice_pdfs`, `bank_reconciliations`, `attachments`, `attachment_parts`) fall under the existing rule.
- Stage 1 (security) is the one stage not done — it needs Firebase Auth / rules changes and a deploy decision.
