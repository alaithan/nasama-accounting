## 1. Commission / VAT — per-deal VAT-inclusive mode + deal↔invoice guards

**Commit:** `58ddd05` · 4 files, +471 / −28

### The report
Deal **The View Island · TVI-126** — 2,420,000 @ 5% — stored an expected
commission of **115,238.09**, and draft invoice #126 came out at
**120,999.99** incl. VAT. The agreed cheque was **121,000.00**.

### Why it happened

**Cause 1 — no VAT-inclusive mode.** Commission was always treated as
VAT-exclusive: `total = fee + 5%`. There was no way to say *"121,000 is the whole
cheque"*. Entering the commission net of VAT to compensate (115,238.09) then hit
a rounding wall — 5% of 115,238.09 is 5,761.90, so the total settled at
120,999.99 and could never reach 121,000.00.

**Cause 2 — unguarded drift.** `expected_commission_net` is deliberately
hand-editable (imports, negotiated amounts), but nothing ever compared it to
`transaction_value × commission_pct`. Two formulas, never reconciled:

| | formula | for TVI-126 |
|---|---|---|
| Deal | `transaction_value × pct` | 121,000.00 |
| Invoice line | `dealValue × pct`, then + 5% | whatever was typed |

So a drifted deal invoiced a wrong amount silently, and a wrong invoice never
contradicted its deal.

### What changed

**Two VAT modes, per deal.** New `commission_vat_inclusive` field; the deal
form's VAT control is now a three-way choice:

| Mode | 5% of 2,420,000 means | Net | VAT | Invoice total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Added on top *(default)* | the fee | 121,000.00 | 6,050.00 | 127,050.00 |
| Included in commission | the all-in cheque | 115,238.10 | 5,761.90 | **121,000.00** |
| No VAT | the fee | 121,000.00 | — | 121,000.00 |

The load-bearing detail: under INCLUSIVE terms VAT is the **remainder**
(`gross − net`), not 5% of the net. Taking 5% of the net gives 120,999.99 and
misses the agreed cheque by a fils, every time. `expected_commission_net` stays
the NET in both modes, so every report keeps reading one field — the canonical
model from 2026-07-17 is unchanged.

New helpers in `core.jsx` — `isVatInclusive`, `impliedCommissionCents`,
`impliedNetCents`, `dealVatCents`, `dealGrossCents` — replaced **four hardcoded
`× 1.05` sites**. Invoice lines carry a `vatInclusive` flag and split through
`invSplitVat`; the printed invoice states *"Commission is inclusive of 5% VAT"*
so the document is unambiguous about its basis.

**Guards against drift.**

- *Deal form*: an amber warning when the stored net no longer matches what
  value × % implies (mode-aware), with a one-click correction, plus a permanent
  line showing the resulting invoice total.
- *Invoice editor*: a full-width row under any line billing something other than
  its deal's outstanding commission, with a **Match deal** button.
- *Drafts auto-match on open* — a draft is a working document, so its deal-linked
  lines are lined up with the deal automatically. **Issued invoices are never
  repriced**: they are tax records, so a mismatch there is reported only.
- *Issuing confirms first*, listing every offending deal and warning that an
  issued invoice can only be voided, not corrected.
- A deal that contradicts its **own** value × % blocks auto-match entirely and
  offers to fix the deal at source (writes `expected_commission_net` to
  Firestore, then reprices the line off the corrected figure in one click).

**Separate bug found and fixed.** When billing a partial commission, the derived
rate was rounded to 2 dp — `4.761905% → 4.76%` — so the invoice missed the
outstanding amount by **AED 46.09** on a 2.42M deal, and far more on a 200M one.
`invPctForAmount` now keeps the deal's own rate when it already lands on the
target and widens precision only when it must, until the billed amount is exact
to the fils. Invoice VAT is also now summed per line rather than taken as 5% of
the grand total — each line is its own supply.

### Two course-corrections during the work
Both worth recording, because both produced a *worse* intermediate state:

**Built on the wrong VAT assumption first.** Asked which basis applied and was
told "5% + VAT on top", so the first version treated 115,238.09 as a data-entry
error and pushed the deal toward 121,000 + 6,050 = 127,050. The actual
requirement — stated plainly two rounds later — was a 121,000 total. Rather than
swap one hardcoded assumption for the other, both modes were built; the earlier
answer is now simply the default mode.

**Auto-match propagated the bad number.** The first auto-match made the invoice
conform to the deal — but the deal was itself wrong, so issuing reproduced
120,999.99 with a tidier audit trail. Auto-match now stands down when a deal
contradicts its own value × %, and reports it instead of copying it. Conforming
to a wrong source is not agreement, it is a hidden error.

### Notes
- Existing deals have `commission_vat_inclusive` unset, which reads as **on top**
  — the prior behaviour. Nothing changes for any deal until its mode is set.
- The `%` input moved from `step="0.01"` to `step="any"`: a partial bill needs a
  rate finer than 2 dp, and the old step marked those values invalid in-browser.
- Under INCLUSIVE terms the printed `%` column applies to the **all-in** total,
  not the net — so `2,420,000 × 5% = 121,000` ties to the *Total Incl. VAT*
  column. This is why the italic note on the document matters.

### Still open

   auto-corrects to 115,238.10; the stored 115,238.09 was one fils off the exact
   back-out) → save. Reopening the invoice then lines it up at
   115,238.10 + 5,761.90 = **121,000.00**.
2. **Draft #126 still holds the old figures** and needs regenerating after the
   deal is fixed. If a wrong version was ever *issued*, use Delete — for an
   issued invoice that voids rather than deletes, so it stays on file marked Void
   and stops counting as invoiced.
3. **Not yet exercised in a browser.** Verified by transpile and unit tests only.
   The three-way VAT dropdown, the red *fix the deal* row, and the Firestore
   write it performs are the paths to eyeball first.
4. **Other deals may carry the same entry pattern.** If commissions were
   habitually typed net of VAT, those deals are now flagged the moment they are
   opened — worth a pass through the Deals list.

## Net effect
A deal and its invoice can no longer disagree without the app saying so, and an
all-in commission agreement can now be expressed directly instead of being
approximated by a hand-typed net that never quite added up. The rounding that
made 121,000.00 unreachable is gone: under VAT-inclusive terms the total lands on
the agreed cheque exactly.
